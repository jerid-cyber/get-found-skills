# The 4-Phase System

| Phase | Core objective | Primary deliverables | KPIs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Discovery & Assessment | Define the current state, surface constraints, establish a baseline | Audit report, requirements log, gap analysis | Baseline accuracy, stakeholder alignment |
| 2 Architecture & Design | Blueprint the solution, map dependencies, model workflows and risks (FMEA) | Specs, system schema, risk matrix | Sign-off velocity, design test coverage |
| 3 Implementation & Deployment | Build in sprints, QA, launch with a staged rollout | Production assets, migration and launch logs | Uptime, feature completion rate |
| 4 Optimization & Governance | Monitor against baseline, optimize, enforce SOPs and compliance | Analytics dashboard, SOPs, audit schedule | Performance vs. baseline, compliance adherence |

## Rules every skill enforces

1. **No baseline, no build.** Phase 1 records the starting number, or the result can't be proven.
2. **Exposure is not the finish line.** Every skill ends at a revenue signal (call, booking, sale, lead).
3. **Measure the zero-click world.** Where AI answers replace clicks, track brand mentions and Share of Model Voice alongside traffic.
